UAV Command — Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Aviation Safety & Regulatory Disclaimer (CYA)

UAV Command is a supplemental preflight planning and situational awareness utility only. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) or the National Weather Service (NWS).

Telemetry, airspace classifications, ceiling estimates, and waypoint audits are provided "AS IS" without warranty of accuracy or completeness. Under 14 CFR Part 107 and 49 U.S.C. § 44809, the Remote Pilot in Command (PIC) retains sole legal responsibility for verifying official FAA NOTAMs/TFRs, obtaining required LAANC authorizations, and ensuring safe flight operations.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

  • No Developer Servers: UAV Command operates in standalone direct-API mode. We do not operate backend servers and do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.
  • On-Device Storage Only: Your Pilot Name/Callsign, TRUST/Part 107 certificate number, uploaded certificate image, custom clearance margins, and flight logbook entries are stored locally on your device via Android local storage and are never transmitted to us.

3. Location & Third-Party Public APIs

When you search a location or tap GPS, your latitude and longitude are sent directly from your device to public weather and aviation data providers solely to retrieve local forecasts, terrain elevation, and airspace context:

  • Open-Meteo: Weather, wind shear, precipitation, and digital elevation data.
  • National Weather Service (NWS / NOAA): Active weather advisories and solar Kp geomagnetic data.
  • FAA Public ArcGIS / NASR Data: Publicly available UAS Facility Map and airport proximity boundaries.
